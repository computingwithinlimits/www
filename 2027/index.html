LIMITS Hubs

In 2027, LIMITS will be a virtual, distributed workshop. We welcome participants to organize local gatherings or "LIMITS-hubs" that encourage community-building and sharing of infrastructure. Details to be added soon.

Registration

LIMITS 2027 takes place via Zoom. Please register through the following link (available soon):

Attendance is free. Registration is required for all attendees in order for us to plan group activities, to send you the connection link and any schedule updates.


Congratulations again for your accepted paper at LIMITS and thank so 
much for contributing to our workshop series. We hope that you enjoyed 
participating in LIMITS and that you had fruitful discussions at the 
event.

We are in the process of creating the post-proceedings for LIMITS 2025, 
in which we will include all accepted papers. As indicated in our Call 
for Papers, we are creating arXiv proceedings 
(https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_index.html) to have a no-cost, 
permanent, open-access record of LIMITS. We will submit our proceedings 
index once the individual submissions are available on arXiv.

To make this happen, we need you to

1. *Finalise your paper*, taking reviewer comments, discussions at the 
workshop, and, if applicable, remarks from your shepherd into account. 
Make sure you follow the guidelines and formatting requirements from our 
Call for Papers (https://computingwithinlimits.org/2025/#cfp). The page 
limit for post-proceedings paper is 15 pages including references and 
appendices.
    - The *deadline for this is 2025-08-08 AoE*. Please get in touch if 
you need extra time.
    - If your paper is shepherded, please submit an almost-final version 
to your shepherd a few days before the deadline and discuss your changes 
with them.
2. *Submit your finalised paper* to https://arxiv.org/
    - We suggest that you add the following text to the "Comments" field 
of your submission to identify your work as a LIMITS paper: 
"Post-proceedings paper presented at LIMITS 2025: 11th Workshop on 
Computing within Limits, 2025-06-26/27, Online."
    - arXiv papers are categorised and we propose that you categorise 
your papers under "Computer Science" with it's sub-categories. If you 
have difficulties categorizing your paper or if you need to be endorsed 
for submitting in this category 
(https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html), please get in touch with 
us by replying to this email.
    - We also encourage you to select the Creative Commons "CC BY" 
license or the arXiv.org non-exclusive license when submitting your 
paper.
3. A day or two after your paper has been submitted, you will receive a 
notification from arXiv regrading to publication of your paper. You will 
receive an arXiv identifier and a DOI (looking like 
https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23866 and 
https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.23866)
   - Please *send us these identifiers* for your paper by replying to 
this email.
   - If you want, you can add the DOI to your paper (e.g., update 
"\acmDOI{}" to "\acmDOI{10.48550/arXiv.2506.23866}" in your LaTeX code 
to make the DOI appear on the bottom of the PDF) and upload it again to 
arXiv. This will not change identifiers.
